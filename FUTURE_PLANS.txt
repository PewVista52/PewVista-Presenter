PewVista Presenter - Future Plans & Upcoming Features
=====================================================

Thank you for using PewVista Presenter in your church services!

We are continually improving PewVista Presenter. Every upcoming improvement is
included in your regular program updates at no extra cost, and updates always
preserve your church's songs, Bibles, services, members, and custom branding.


UPCOMING FEATURES IN DEVELOPMENT
--------------------------------

1. Dedicated "Connections" Tab (Wired & Wireless Displays Together)
   - Manage all your church's wired and wireless screens from one simple tab.
   - Wired Screens (HDMI, VGA, DisplayPort, USB-C):
     - Automatic detection of all plugged-in projectors, TVs, and monitors.
     - One-click "Set Screens to Extend" button so operators never have to
       adjust Windows display settings manually.
     - "Identify Screens" button to quickly see which screen is which.
   - Wireless Projectors:
     - One-click wireless projector scanning and connection directly inside
       PewVista Presenter.
     - Wi-Fi link and scannable QR code for Smart/Android projectors on the
       church Wi-Fi network.

2. Multiple Wireless Secondary Screens (Android TVs, Tablets, and Phones)
   - Connect additional Android Smart TVs, tablets, or phones over your church
     Wi-Fi (or the PC's built-in mobile hotspot—no internet required) to serve
     as extra live secondary screens for the choir, stage, or overflow rooms.
   - All wired projectors and wireless screens stay in sync automatically.

3. Wireless Operator Remote Control (Android Phone or Tablet)
   - Control your service wirelessly from an Android phone or tablet using a
     secure operator PIN and QR code.
   - View the live preview, advance items in the Display Queue, and send songs,
     Bible passages, welcome notes, and preparation items from anywhere in the
     sanctuary.

4. Standalone Android Edition (Future Phase)
   - A dedicated Android edition for churches that want to run presentations
     directly from an Android tablet or phone without a PC, while staying
     compatible with PewVista Presenter database backups.
