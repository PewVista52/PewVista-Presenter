PewVista Presenter - Future Plans & Upcoming Features
=====================================================

Thank you for using PewVista Presenter in your church services!

We are continually improving PewVista Presenter. Every upcoming improvement is
included in your regular program updates at no extra cost, and updates always
preserve your church's songs, Bibles, services, members, and custom branding.


UPCOMING PHASES IN DEVELOPMENT
------------------------------

PHASE 1: Musician Keys, Sub Secondary Screen & Screen Connections
- Musical Keys in the Song Library ("Insert Keys"):
  - Add musical chords/keys directly above words for every verse and chorus in
    your Song Library, with quick-pick chord buttons and one-click Transpose
    Up (+1) and Down (-1).
  - Fast database optimization to handle libraries of 1,000 to 5,000+ songs
    smoothly while preserving all your existing songs.
- Dedicated "Sub Secondary Screen" (Musician & Choir Monitor):
  - A live stage/musician screen alongside the main congregation Secondary
    Screen.
  - When a song is live on the main projector (with background), the Sub
    Secondary Screen automatically displays the same verse on a clean,
    high-contrast background with the musical keys above the words, plus a
    preview of the upcoming line and next song key.
- Settings > Screen Connections (Wired & Wireless Together):
  - Manage all wired displays (HDMI, VGA, DisplayPort, USB-C), wireless
    projectors, and wireless Android screens in one place with live Green/Red
    connection status indicators.
  - One-click "Set Screens to Extend" and "Identify Screens" buttons.
  - Assign any connected wired or wireless screen to Main Screen, Secondary
    Screen, Sub Secondary Screen, or Private Screen, and save fixed church
    devices so they reconnect automatically every time the program opens.
- Printable Connection Barcodes (Print > Barcodes):
  - Print permanent QR/connection barcodes for your church's screens (matched
    across both PCs on your church license) so musicians and operators can scan
    once to connect securely over church Wi-Fi or local offline hotspot without
    internet traffic on personal devices.


PHASE 2: Wireless Operator Remote Control & Flexible Screen Licensing
- Wireless Operator Remote Control (Android Phone or Tablet):
  - Control the entire PewVista Presenter GUI wirelessly from an authorized
    Android phone or tablet using a secure operator barcode and PIN.
  - Manage the Display Queue, view the live preview, turn screens On/Off,
    search and send Songs, Bible passages, Welcome & Birthdays, and Preparation
    items, save/load services, and send quiet stage alerts from anywhere in the
    building.
- Flexible, Modular Subscription Pricing Based on Your Church's Setup:
  - Choose only the services and screen counts your church needs:
    - Number of Fixed Secondary Screens
    - Number of Fixed Sub Secondary Screens (musician & choir monitors)
    - Number of Wireless Devices added
    - Wireless Operator Remote Control option (control the full GUI from a phone/tablet)
    - Private Screen option (preacher & podium monitor)


PHASE 3: Dedicated "Private Screen" (Preacher & Podium Monitor)
- Full build-out of the new Private Screen tab and display output for the
  pulpit or stage podium.
- Displays preacher notes, service clock/timer, current slide and next slide
  preview, and quiet operator messages on a wired monitor or wireless Android
  tablet.


PHASE 4: Bible Store & Cross-Platform Bible Upgrades
- Upgraded Bible importing and built-in options for obtaining and adding more
  Bibles easily.
- Cross-platform Bible and database support ready for both Windows PCs and
  Android devices.


FUTURE EXPANSION: Standalone Android Edition (No PC Required)
- A dedicated Android edition for churches that want to run PewVista Presenter
  directly from an Android tablet or phone as the main hub without a PC, fully
  compatible with your Windows song, Bible, and service backups.
- Flexible Multi-Device Licensing: Choose 1 or more main devices (Windows PCs
  or Android devices) on your church license and add extra devices anytime as
  your setup grows.
